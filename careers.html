Working here

What it's like

Working at Ninety Utilities means being part of a smaller, hands-on team delivering planned gas infrastructure work to the standards expected by major clients and principal contractors.

We expect people to work safely, communicate properly and take responsibility for the quality of what they do.

In return, we aim to provide the right equipment, clear supervision, access to the information needed to do the job properly and support for maintaining and developing the competence required for the role.

Our teams also use digital systems on site, giving them access to relevant project information, procedures, Construction Phase Plans, inspections and reporting tools directly through their apps.

If something needs reporting, it can be raised there and then. If information changes, the latest version is available without waiting for paperwork to make its way back to site.

We are interested in people who want to do the job properly, contribute to the team and build their experience as the business grows.
