Systems

Digital management & assurance

Our safety and management systems are digital.

Ninety Utilities operates a central business platform that brings together our Safety Management System, management systems, workforce information, competence records, project documentation, inspections, incidents and performance data.

It gives the business one live source of information rather than relying on disconnected spreadsheets, paper files and documents sitting in different places.

Information where the work happens

Our teams have access through dedicated apps, putting the information relevant to their role and project directly in their hands.

That means current procedures, project information, briefings, risk controls and other relevant documents can be accessed from site without relying on a paper folder or somebody in the office sending the latest version.

Digital Construction Phase Plans

Construction Phase Plans are managed digitally and kept relevant to the work being delivered.

Project information, responsibilities, significant risks, arrangements and supporting documentation can be maintained as the work develops, helping ensure the CPP remains a working project document rather than something produced at mobilisation and then forgotten.

Relevant information is available to the people who need it throughout delivery.

Report it there and then

If something happens on site, reporting should not have to wait until somebody gets back to a desk.

Incidents, near misses, hazards and other safety observations can be reported directly through our digital system, allowing information to be captured quickly and escalated to the right people.

Actions, investigation and close-out can then be tracked through the same system, creating a clear record from the initial report through to completion.

Inspections in real time

Site inspections and assurance activity are recorded digitally at the point of delivery.

Findings are available as soon as the inspection is submitted, giving management immediate visibility of what is happening across active works.

Actions can be assigned, tracked and closed out within the platform, providing an auditable record without waiting for inspection forms to be returned and manually entered.

Live performance data

Our key performance indicators are tracked through the same platform, giving us a current view of safety, operational and management performance across the business.

Rather than waiting for information to be collated at the end of a reporting period, trends, outstanding actions and areas requiring attention can be identified as the data is recorded.

For clients and principal contractors, it means the information behind our management systems is not simply written policy — it is being captured, monitored and used as the work happens.
